Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide Spendly, we collect only what we need:

  • Account details: your name, email address and an encrypted password.
  • Expense data you enter: amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical data, such as browser type, to keep the service working.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to run your account, show your expenses, and build your spending summaries and category breakdowns. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected with reasonable security measures. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your expense records are deleted too.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law. Pages may load fonts from external providers such as Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information like your IP address.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access or delete your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
